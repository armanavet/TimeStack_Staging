About Timestack

Timestack is a daily ordering puzzle: you’re given a small deck of events, discoveries or cultural moments, and your job is to arrange them from earliest to latest. Get the whole stack right to solve the day’s puzzle.

This page is a local clone of the game page. The puzzle itself is served from the bundled Build folder rather than the live site, so it runs entirely from these files.
